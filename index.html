The Day’s Program

  1. 7:00 AM – 7:30 AMArrival & Gathering
    • Guests arrive, get settled in, and find their seats.
    • Light morning coffee and drinks available.
  2. 7:30 AM – 8:00 AMOpening Prayer & Welcome Remarks
    • Ecumenical opening prayer.
    • Welcome addresses from family elders and the host committee.
  3. 8:00 AM – 9:30 AMIcebreakers & Family Roll Call
    • Branch Roll Call: cheering and introduction per family branch.
    • Quick icebreaker games, name games, and family trivia.
  4. 9:30 AM – 11:30 AMMorning Family Games & Activities
    • Kids Division: egg toss, sack race, and mini-challenges.
    • Adult & Senior Division: Family Bingo and inter-branch relay games.
  5. 11:30 AM – 12:00 PMFamily Photo Session
    • Grand family portrait with all attendees.
    • Generational photos: Seniors, Adults, Youth, and Kids.
    • Individual family branch photos.
  6. 12:00 PM – 1:30 PMBuffet Lunch & Open Mic
    • Full buffet lunch serving.
    • Open stage for casual toasts, greetings, and sharing nostalgic memories.
  7. 1:30 PM – 2:30 PMRest, Siesta & Free Time
    • Free time for senior rest, kid free-play, and casual social catch-ups.
    • Vintage photo gallery display area open.
  8. 2:30 PM – 3:45 PMAfternoon Group Challenges & Karaoke
    • Open-stage family karaoke.
    • Parlor charades, giant board games, and group talent performances.
  9. 3:45 PM – 4:30 PMAfternoon Snacks (Merienda)
    • Refreshments, afternoon coffee, desserts, and local pastries served.
  10. 4:30 PM – 4:45 PMPassing of the Host Ceremony
    • Symbolic handoff of the reunion torch/banner to the next host branch.
    • Acceptance remarks and teaser from the upcoming host family.
  11. 4:45 PM – 5:00 PMClosing Prayer & Farewell
    • Final blessing and closing prayer.
    • Distribution of family souvenirs and tokens.
    • Photo wrap-up, and safe travels home.
